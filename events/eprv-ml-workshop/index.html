EPRV & Machine Learning Workshop

Event Details

Dates: Tuesday, June 11th, 2024

Time: 8:00am - 11:30am Pacific Time

Location: Zoom

Description

This workshop focused on the synergies between EPRV and Machine Learning and consisted of three segments: overview talks on EPRV and Machine Learning, science talks on current EPRV research efforts that leverage ML techniques, and a discussion panel on promising future directions in this area.
