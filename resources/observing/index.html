Observing Resources

Resources to help the EPRV community prepare competitive observing proposals and identify which instruments are available to apply for. Contributions are welcome — email eprv-rcn.leads [at] jpl.nasa.gov or reach out on Slack if you have materials to add.

Proposal Writing

On September 10, 2026, the RCN hosted a digital workshop on writing more compelling EPRV observing proposals, with speakers from NOIRLab, ESO, NEID, ESPRESSO, MAROON-X, and more. The workshop recording is available on YouTube, and slides from selected segments are linked below. See also the workshop event page for the original agenda.

General guidelines

Best practices for framing science cases, choosing instruments and observing strategies, and presenting a clear technical case to TACs. Community-contributed notes and links will be collected here over time.

  • RCN workshop recording: How to Write Compelling Observing Proposals (September 10, 2026)
  • Guide to Writing Strong Proposals for the HET (Jason Wright & Mike Eracleous, May 2024)

Figures and captions

Guidance on designing clear proposal figures and writing captions that support your science case.

  • Figures and Captions slides from the RCN workshop (PDF)
  • Proposal Figure Checklist — points to consider when designing and finalizing figures (PDF)

Simulating EPRV data

Tools and examples for generating synthetic RV datasets to support feasibility arguments and survey design in proposals.

  • Alejandro's data simulation tutorial slides from the RCN workshop (PDF)
  • Tutorial notebook on using Oscar Barragan's Pyaneti to simulate EPRV data (link)

Additional notebooks or scripts that generate synthetic RV data with public code/ packages are welcome. If you are willing to share an example (or record a short walkthrough for the RCN YouTube channel), contact the RCN leads.


Exposure time calculators

Links and notes on finding and using exposure time calculators (ETCs) for individual EPRV instruments.

  • NEID ETC Website & BitBucket repo
  • KPF ETC github repo
  • ESPRESSO ETC Website
  • HARPS ETC Website
  • NIRPS ETC Website
  • CARMENES ETC Website

Contributions needed: if you maintain or regularly use an instrument ETC, please share the link and a short usage note with the RCN leads so we can list it here.


Examples of successful proposals

PDFs of previously accepted observing proposals (shared with the authors' permission) to illustrate what competitive proposals can look like across different TACs.
